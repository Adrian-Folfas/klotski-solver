Partitions on cols, rows constructor inputs:
    - cols = 1, rows > 1
    - cols > 1, rows = 1
    - cols, rows both > 1

Partitions on exit_squares constructor input:
    - Empty list
    - Some number of Coordinates

Partitions on is_in_bounds:
    - coord is in bounds
    - coord is one step out of bounds
    - coord is far out of bounds
